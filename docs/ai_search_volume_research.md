# AI Search Volume Research — Fallback Percentage Justification

## Key Data Points (as of June/July 2026)

- AI Overviews appear on ~48–55% of ALL Google searches (Ahrefs/WordStream, 2026)
- AI Overviews on LOCAL queries: 7% (Ahrefs — "near me" style) vs 40–43% (broader local-intent queries like "best HVAC in Fredericksburg VA")
- AI Mode as % of total Google searches: ~1–2% (Semrush, mid-2025)
- ChatGPT volume vs Google: ~12% of Google's monthly volume (Ahrefs, Feb 2026)
- Gemini: ~5–8% of Google's volume (estimated)
- 35%+ of US consumers have used AI to find a local business (GrowthPro AI, June 2026)
- 88% of local businesses have no GEO strategy

## Fallback Decision

Use **25% of Google search volume** when DataForSEO AI-specific volume returns null/zero.

Reasoning:
- Conversational geo-specific queries (our style) trigger AI Overviews ~40% of the time
- ChatGPT adds ~12% of Google volume on top
- Gemini adds ~5–8%
- Overlap between platforms means blended estimate is 20–30%
- 25% is conservative, defensible, and slightly undersells the real number

## Report Disclaimer Text

"AI search volume estimates based on available data. Where direct AI search data is unavailable, estimates reflect approximately 25% of Google search volume — consistent with current AI search adoption rates for local service queries."
